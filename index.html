About Us

Who We Are

M/S KTL Concrete Hollow Block Industries is a leading manufacturer of high-quality concrete hollow blocks, committed to providing durable and reliable building materials for construction projects of all sizes.

With state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and a dedicated team of professionals, we produce hollow blocks that meet industry standards for strength, durability, and precision. Our blocks are widely used in residential, commercial, and industrial construction.

We take pride in delivering consistent quality, competitive pricing, and timely supply to our valued customers.
